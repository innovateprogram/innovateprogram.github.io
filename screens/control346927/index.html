Welcome to your screen controls

Follow these simple steps to put your first update on the display.

  1. 1
    Add a slide

    Choose Image, Video, or YouTube, then add your media. You can also create a photo story from several pictures.

  2. 2
    Choose how it appears

    Set a heading and description, hide the text, or show supported media full-screen. Choose how long slides stay on screen.

  3. 3
    Save and check the display

    Select Add slide. Your changes publish automatically. Use View screen to check what visitors see.

  4. 4
    Manage playback and notices

    Use the controls on the right to pause automatic slides or show a short visitor notice.
