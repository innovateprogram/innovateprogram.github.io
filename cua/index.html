Why C.U.A

The environment isn't a debate. It's a deadline.

At C.U.A, we know protecting the environment is the right call — not a talking point. One of the biggest problems facing our environment right now is the never-ending stream of rubbish flowing into it.

Our plan starts with the basics: better bin systems, clearer sorting, and real education so households can put better habits into practice every single week.

Our Policies

Three ways we sort this out.

We're prioritising the environment with a plan built on consistency, reduction, and protection — the same three things every good bin system needs.

Consistency

One national colour code

Every bin, the same colour, in every state — no more guessing which lid means what when you cross a border.

Reduction

Less single-use, more reusable

We'll cut single-use plastic at the source and back reusable products so there's simply less to throw away.

Protection

Stronger local protections

Funded community clean-ups, better collection routes, and tougher litter laws to protect the places we live.

C.U.A In Action

Two changes you'll actually notice.

Policy 01

Weekly collection, every week

We'll pick up bins every week instead of the current fortnightly schedule. That means fewer overflowing bins, less illegal dumping, and less rubbish piling up at home — plus a tidier-looking street for every neighbourhood.

Policy 02

Free monthly bin cleaning

A new, free bin-cleaning service to keep every household's waste bins hygienic — cutting down on food build-up, mould, and bad smells, and reducing maggots and pests along the way.

The Facts
2.5MTonnes of plastic never recycled

2.5 million tonnes of plastic go unrecycled every year. That's why C.U.A is prioritising better waste collection and stronger recycling systems built to stop material from escaping in the first place.

We'll also work with businesses to move toward recyclable packaging — and make sure what goes in the yellow bin actually gets recycled.
